# Welcome! 

My name is Allen and I'm a student at the University of Michigan. I love building applications involving AI. Whether this is training machine learning models (+ building architecture), building applied AI projects (like enterprise RAG, playwright automations, data engineering), or working on development close to the metal (such as low latency C network protocols for agents), I worked extensively in building the right intuition for how AI works in the real world. 

My current programming stack focuses heavily on C (and C++ 23) and Python. I am learning more about Rust at the moment but I use those two languages on a daily basis. Besides programming, you can find me in a small bookshop looking for classic literature, keeping up with geopolitics, or finding niche natural spots to explore. I take a lot of my inspirations of my programming work from the real world and apply it to build what I can imagine. I also have a good sense of when to think big picture and when does small details matter a lot (hence how I recognize how to work with AI well).
